Hello! Welcome to my Golf Game! This is a two stage area, complete with a golf pro shop and three hole mini golf course! My goal with this project was to give players a calm area to explore, while also including some options for gameplay. 
The additional skill that I worked on was the physics of the golf ball and club mechanics. Previously I had not made interactible objects, and now I have objects that interact differently depending on what they are touching (see below)
